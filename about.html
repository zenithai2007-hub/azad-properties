About Us

A family business, built on trust

Azad Properties is a subsidiary of Azad Brothers, established in Istanbul in 2012.

Our story

Azad Brothers began as a family business helping foreigners settle, invest and do business in Türkiye. What started with a handful of clients in Şişli grew into a group of companies covering real estate, legal, tourism, media and trade — but the way we work never changed: one family looking after another, from the first phone call to the title deed and beyond.

Today Azad Properties is the group's real-estate arm. We source homes across Istanbul, guide citizenship and residence applications, manage rentals, and handle resale — so a client never has to chase five different companies for one purchase.
