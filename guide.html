The purchase process

  1. Choose & view
    Tell us your budget and goal. We send a shortlist, then show you the properties in person — or by video tour if you cannot travel yet.
  2. Reserve & contract
    Sign a reservation agreement with a deposit to lock the price, then a formal sales contract with the seller or developer.
  3. Tax number & bank account
    We help you get a Turkish tax number and open a bank account — usually within a day, with your passport.
  4. Transfer & Tapu
    Transfer funds to your Turkish account, complete the valuation and currency-exchange certificate, and sign at the Land Registry (Tapu) office.
  5. Title deed & aftercare
    Receive your Tapu. We then handle utilities, furnishing, rental or residence and citizenship steps — whatever your plan needs.

Costs & fees

  • Title Deed Transfer Tax (Tapu Harcı) — 4% of the declared value, typically split 2% buyer / 2% seller.
  • Annual property tax — 0.1% to 0.2% for residential property (0.2% to 0.4% in metropolitan areas like Istanbul).
  • Valuation & official fees — the SPK appraisal, notary and translation costs are official fees; we quote them in advance, in writing.
  • Legal & advisory — agreed with you before we start, never a surprise.
  • No hidden fees — the price we quote is the price you pay, in USD.

Title deed (Tapu)

The Tapu is your proof of ownership, issued by the Land Registry (Tapu Dairesi). Before transfer we check the title for debts, liens and planning issues. For citizenship investments the Tapu carries a mandatory 3-year non-sale annotation (3 Yıl Satılamaz Şerhi) — you keep full rental rights during those three years.

Power of Attorney

You do not need to be in Türkiye for every step. A limited Power of Attorney, signed at a Turkish notary with a sworn translator, lets our legal counsel handle land registry transactions, tax filings and citizenship procedures on your behalf. The PoA is limited to the property and the procedures you name — nothing more.

Finance & banking

Open a Turkish bank account with your passport and tax number, transfer funds in USD or EUR, and your bank converts them to Turkish Lira with an official Foreign Exchange Purchase Certificate (DAB) — a required document for the Tapu transfer and the citizenship file. We guide every transfer so the paperwork is correct the first time.

Living in Türkiye

  • Car: foreign-plated cars can stay up to 730 days in Türkiye. They must not be driven by anyone other than the owner, and must leave before the period expires.
  • Pets: bring your pets with a veterinary health certificate and a valid rabies vaccination. Animals must be at least 3 months old, limited to two per person.
  • Schools & healthcare: Istanbul has international schools and private hospitals with English-speaking staff — ask us and we will point you to the ones near your property.
  • Daily life: residence permits, utilities, internet and address registration are all handled by our team after your purchase.
